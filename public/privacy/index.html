Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026. NagarTrace is a student-built working prototype (Team Grexars), not a government service. This page describes what the product actually does with data today.

What we collect

  • Sign-in: your email address is handled by Supabase Auth (ap-south-1 region) to sign you in by password or email link. If Google sign-in is enabled, Google shares your name and email with Supabase Auth. The NagarTrace database itself stores only an issuer/subject identifier, a generated or chosen public alias (which may be your real name if you choose it), your optional ward, points and account creation time - not your email or name.
  • Reports you file: the issue type and note you write, GPS location with accuracy, the time, and the photo you attach.
  • Activity: points, badges and - when enabled - streaks, all derived from your activity and shown under your public alias.

Who can see what

  • Public issue board: issue type, status, severity, ward, deadline and counts, with location rounded to about 11 metres. Never your identity, your photos or your notes.
  • Leaderboard (signed in): alias, ward and points only.
  • Photos: visible only to the reporter/uploader, responsible organisation staff and assigned reviewers. Staff handling the issue see its exact location.
  • Email address: never shown publicly.

Photos and metadata

  • The report form can check photo metadata (time, GPS, camera) on your device before upload; that check never leaves your browser.
  • On upload the server re-encodes photos as JPEG and drops EXIF metadata. Faces and number plates are not blurred - do not upload photos you are not comfortable sharing with the issue's reviewers.

Where data lives

  • Database and private photo bucket: Supabase. API: Render. Website: GitHub Pages.
  • Your sign-in session token is kept in your browser's local storage. This site sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

What we do not do

  • No complaint is sent to any real authority from this app.
  • No third-party AI processes your photos.
  • No marketing emails - email is used only for sign-in and account verification.
  • No sale of personal data.

Records and deletion

  • Points and status history are append-only audit records.
  • There is no self-service deletion yet. To request deletion of your account or a report, email the contact below; requests are handled manually with no guaranteed timeline.
  • Test data is kept in a hidden test cohort and never shown on public boards.
